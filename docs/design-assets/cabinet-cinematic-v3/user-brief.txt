Create a premium, cinematic, scroll-driven 3D storytelling website for a professional **Kitchen Cabinet Painting Service**.

The website should visually demonstrate the entire cabinet refinishing process as the visitor scrolls down the page.

The main concept is:

**“Transform an old kitchen into a beautifully refinished modern kitchen — step by step.”**

The experience should feel like the user is watching the kitchen renovation happen in real time while scrolling.

---

# 1. Overall Experience

Create a sophisticated, high-end website with:

- 3D scroll storytelling
- Smooth scroll-triggered animations
- Cinematic transitions
- Realistic kitchen cabinet visuals
- Premium residential renovation branding
- Minimal but elegant typography
- High-end interior design aesthetic
- Strong before-and-after transformation
- Clear service explanation
- Strong conversion-focused CTA

The experience should feel similar to a premium architectural visualization or luxury interior-design website rather than a normal contractor website.

Use a combination of:

- Three.js / WebGL
- GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scrolling
- CSS animations
- React / Next.js
- Responsive WebGL rendering

Optimize animations carefully so the website remains fast and smooth on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

---

# 2. Main 3D Scroll Storytelling Concept

The main section should stay visually pinned while the visitor scrolls.

Place a realistic 3D kitchen in the center of the screen.

At the beginning, show an **older kitchen with dated or worn cabinet finishes**.

As the user scrolls, progressively transform the cabinets through each stage of the professional painting process.

The kitchen camera can subtly move, zoom, orbit, or shift perspective between stages.

Each scroll section should feel connected to the previous one.

Do NOT simply fade between unrelated images.

The visitor should feel like they are watching the same kitchen being professionally transformed.

# 4. Scroll Story – Stage 01
## Initial Inspection

Transition to the original kitchen before work begins.

Show slightly dated cabinet doors and finishes.

Highlight several cabinet areas with subtle 3D markers.

Text:

**01 — Inspection & Preparation**

Before painting begins, we inspect the cabinet surfaces, hardware, coatings, and overall condition to determine the correct preparation system.

Visual interaction:

- Subtle camera inspection movement
- Cabinet doors highlighted individually
- Small callout markers
- Soft lighting
- Professional, technical feeling

---

# 5. Scroll Story – Stage 02
## Remove Doors & Hardware

As scrolling continues, cabinet doors should smoothly move away from the cabinet frames in 3D.

Handles and hinges can separate as individual components.

Text:

**02 — Doors & Hardware Removed**

Cabinet doors, drawers, handles, hinges, and removable hardware are carefully labelled and removed before refinishing begins.

Animation ideas:

- Cabinet doors slide outward
- Hinges detach
- Handles separate
- Components move into an organized layout beside the kitchen
- Labels subtly appear

Keep the animation clean and premium, not cartoonish.

---

# 6. Scroll Story – Stage 03
## Cleaning & Degreasing

Create a visual treatment showing the cabinet surfaces being professionally cleaned.

Text:

**03 — Deep Cleaning & Degreasing**

Kitchen cabinets accumulate grease, oils, cooking residue, and contaminants that can prevent coatings from bonding correctly.

We thoroughly clean and degrease every surface before sanding.

Visual ideas:

- Slight surface grime visible initially
- Cleaning effect moves across cabinet panels
- Surface becomes visibly cleaner
- Close-up camera transition showing surface texture

---

# 7. Scroll Story – Stage 04
## Sanding

Transition into a close-up of one cabinet door.

Show a subtle sanding process.

Text:

**04 — Surface Preparation**

Cabinet surfaces are carefully sanded to remove imperfections, reduce gloss, and create the correct surface profile for maximum coating adhesion.

Animation:

- Camera zooms toward cabinet surface
- Fine sanding effect moves across panel
- Gloss level gradually reduces
- Surface texture becomes smooth and uniform

Avoid excessive dust effects.

The visual should remain clean and professional.

---

# 8. Scroll Story – Stage 05
## Masking & Protection

Pull the camera back to reveal the kitchen.

Protect surrounding areas.

Text:

**05 — Masking & Protection**

Benchtops, floors, appliances, walls, splashbacks, and surrounding areas are carefully protected before coatings are applied.

Animation:

- Protective sheets appear over benchtops
- Floor protection rolls out
- Appliances become masked
- Painter's tape traces around edges
- Kitchen transforms into a controlled painting environment

This scene should communicate professionalism and attention to detail.

---

# 9. Scroll Story – Stage 06
## Primer Application

Create the first major visual transformation.

Show cabinet surfaces gradually changing as primer is applied.

Text:

**06 — Professional Primer**

A specialist bonding primer is applied to create a durable foundation and improve adhesion between the existing cabinet surface and the new finish.

Animation:

A smooth coating passes across cabinet surfaces.

Old cabinet color → uniform primer finish.

Show a subtle cross-section or microscopic coating layer visualization if appropriate.

Optional educational label:

Existing Surface  
→ Prepared Surface  
→ Bonding Primer  
→ Finish Coats

# 10. Scroll Story – Stage 07
## Professional Spray Finish

This should be one of the most visually impressive moments of the website.

Text:

**07 — Premium Spray Finish**

Multiple professional finish coats are applied to create a smooth, durable, factory-like finish.

Show the selected new cabinet color gradually covering the primer.

Animation:

- Spray mist moves smoothly across cabinet door
- Cabinet color transitions progressively
- Surface becomes perfectly uniform
- Subtle light reflection demonstrates smooth finish
- Camera moves along cabinet edges and profiles

Make the finish feel refined and premium.

Avoid unrealistic exaggerated paint effects.

---

# 11. Scroll Story – Stage 08

Slow the animation slightly.

Text:

Visual concept:

Show subtle time progression.

Possible visual indicators:

Coating Applied  
→ Drying

→ Ready for Installation

# 20. Animation Direction

Animations should feel:

- Slow
- Controlled
- Precise
- Premium
- Architectural
- Physically believable

Avoid:

- Excessive bouncing
- Cartoon-style animation
- Fast spinning objects
- Heavy particle effects
- Unnecessary parallax
- Distracting UI movement

Use scroll progress to control the 3D timeline.

Scrolling forward should progress through the renovation.

Scrolling backwards should naturally reverse the animation.

---

# 21. Performance Requirements

The experience must remain performant.

Use:

- GLTF / GLB models
- Draco compression
- WebP / AVIF textures
- Lazy loading
- Progressive asset loading
- GPU-friendly materials
- Optimized geometry
- Responsive texture resolution

Desktop can use the full 3D experience.

Tablet should use a simplified 3D version.

Mobile should preserve the storytelling concept while reducing GPU-heavy effects.

Target:

60 FPS where reasonably possible.

# Final Objective

The website should NOT simply explain cabinet painting.

It should **visually demonstrate why professional cabinet painting requires multiple preparation and coating stages**.

By the time the visitor reaches the bottom of the page, they should clearly understand:

1. What happens to their kitchen.
2. Why professional preparation matters.
3. How the painting process works.
4. What the finished result can look like.
5. Why refinishing can be an alternative to replacing cabinetry.
6. How to request a quote.

The overall experience should feel like:

**“Watching your own kitchen transformation happen while you scroll.”**
