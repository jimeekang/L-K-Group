# Current module boundaries

- `service-catalog/domain` owns the four service identifiers and catalogue shape. It is pure TypeScript. `application/list-services` applies the agreed display order to a supplied catalogue; the public entry point supplies local content from `infrastructure`.
- `service-catalog/presentation` renders the supplied catalogue and the static Kitchen Cabinet Painting detail. Service routes, icons and contact anchors are UI concerns, separate from the domain. Cabinet categories, scope, process, example briefs and FAQs live in `service-catalog/content`. Company-provided methods, product family and general timing are distinguished from unconfirmed package inclusions, prices and project-specific arrangements; the page does not sell fixed packages.
- `company-profile` owns company copy, confirmed contact details, business identifiers, service-area content, page navigation and the related sections. Cabinet content reuses company facts through its public entry point. Its shared header and footer use page context to link back to home sections from a detail page. Only the mobile menu is a Client Component. Source and outstanding assets are recorded in [the company brief](../../docs/reference-assets/company-brief.md).
- `showcase` owns the gallery of company-provided cabinet preparation and spray-painting photos. It does not invent project locations or before-and-after pairs. Company promotional artwork is displayed in About; generated Cabinet example images remain explicitly labelled as concepts.
- `src/app` composes public module entry points and owns route metadata. Module internals use relative imports; imports from another module go through its `index.ts`.
- `src/shared/ui` contains content-neutral layout and links. Central image paths, dimensions and alt text are in `src/shared/config/assets.ts`; navigation targets and prototype settings are separate configuration.

There are no database repositories, external adapters, forms, prices, bookings or commerce workflows yet. Add domain/application/infrastructure layers only when they have an actual role. Phone and email links open the visitor's chosen contact application; they do not create a customer record or confirm a successful enquiry. Cabinet online Free Quote buttons remain natively disabled, with working links to the contact section as an alternative.
